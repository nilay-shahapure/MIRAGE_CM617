## Printed p.118 (PDF 130), Section 7.2.7, Table 7.2 Runoff Coefficients of Common Types of Roofs

"The coefficient of runoff varies depending on the type of roof and indicates the fraction of rainwater that can be collected from roof. Run-off coefficients for common types of roofs are shown in Table 7.2."
Table 7.2: GI Sheet 0.9; Asbestos 0.8; Tiled 0.75; Concrete 0.7.
"Water available (in litres) = Annual rainfall (in mm) x Roof area (in sq.m) x Runoff Coefficient"
Worked example (p.119): "Water available from roof top = Annual rainfall (in mm) X Area of roof (in sq.m) X Coefficient of runoff for the roof = 1000 X 20 X 0.75 = 15000 liters"

## Printed p.123 (PDF 135), Section 7.6 Economic Viability

"A typical domestic roof top rainwater harvesting system requires and investment of about Rs.12,000/- to Rs.16,000/-, depending on the capacity of the storage tank. This works out to Rs.2.34 to Rs.1.49 per litre of water stored."
"It is advisable to have the user household themselves meet a sizeable portion of the cost of RRHS to ensure its sustainability and replicability. This would also encourage ownership and appropriate maintenance of the system at the level of households."

## Printed p.166 and p.170 (PDF 178, 182), Table 9.4 Format with an Example of Financial Analysis of Artificial Recharge scheme

Scheme life: "Life of the scheme 15 years". Interest rate on loan 11.5%. "Establishment charges @ 1% of cost of percolation tank"; "O&M of percolation Tank @ 2% of cost of Percolation tank". Economic analysis: "Discount Rate : 15%". Estimation of Economic Water Rate: "Equated Instalment", annual energy cost, establishment 1%, O&M 2%, "Total Annual Cost", divided by annual discharge gives "Water Rate/cu m : Rs. 1.60" (Rs 1.08 with full subsidy on establishment and O&M).

Use in MIRAGE: O&M taken as 2% of capital cost per year (CGWB norm), design life 15 years (CGWB example) with 20 years as sensitivity, levelised cost = annualised capital + O&M, divided by annual yield.
